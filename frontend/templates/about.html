How it works

TomatoSense looks at the colour and appearance of your tomato and tells you whether it's ripe or still green.

1
You upload a photo

Take a picture of one tomato, or choose one from your gallery.

2
We compare it to real tomatoes

The photo is checked against hundreds of ripe and unripe tomato photos it has learned from.

3
You get an answer

Ripe or unripe, plus how sure we are. The photo is analysed instantly and never stored.

Good to know

  • {{ icons.icon("chevron") }}It works best with a single tomato in good light on a plain background.
  • {{ icons.icon("chevron") }}It only answers ripe or unripe — it can't spot damage, rot, or tell varieties apart.
  • {{ icons.icon("chevron") }}Unusual colours (yellow or striped varieties, strong lighting) can throw it off.
  • {{ icons.icon("chevron") }}Your photos are processed on the spot and never saved.
